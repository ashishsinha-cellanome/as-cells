# Comprehensive Cell-Line Cluster Analysis & Finetuning Strategy (Cell-Adhered Class)

This report provides a critical analysis of morphological clusters across 16 cell-adhered datasets. We evaluate embeddings generated by **RF-DETR** (a discriminatively fine-tuned model) and **DINOv2** (a self-supervised foundational model) using a comprehensive suite of distance metrics: **MMD-RBF, FID, L2-Var-Norm, Var-Weighted Cosine, and Coverage Distance**.

Our objective is to decode the true topological structure of these cell lines, identify redundant data, and define an optimal, minimal set of cell lines for zero-shot generalization training.

---

## 1. DINOv2 vs. RF-DETR: The Paradigm Shift in Feature Spaces

A fundamental difference emerges when observing how DINOv2 and RF-DETR structure their embedding spaces:

*   **RF-DETR (Task-Specific Discriminator):** Because RF-DETR is heavily fine-tuned for semantic segmentation and bounding-box regression, its feature space is highly "opinionated" and block-diagonal. It aggressively pushes apart cell lines with distinct morphological boundaries (e.g., separating `DC` and `astrocytes` entirely from `fibroblasts`). RF-DETR forms tight, highly structured clusters.
*   **DINOv2 (Generative Foundational Blob):** DINOv2 maps features based on universal, self-supervised visual similarities. Its 768-D space is much more continuous and entangled. DINOv2 sees the "universal cellular texture" in almost all cell lines, causing datasets to blur together at broader scales (e.g., $K=30$). It acts as an excellent generic feature extractor but struggles to assert hard boundaries between fundamentally distinct biological domains.

---

## 2. Nuances of the Distance Metrics

The choice of metric heavily dictates the perceived clusters. We observed a stark contrast between **Global Statistical metrics** and **Geometric Overlap metrics**:

### The "Global Fallacy": MMD-RBF, L2-Var-Norm, and Var-Weighted Cosine
*   **Behavior:** These metrics measure the macroscopic shape of a dataset (mean, variance, covariance). 
*   **The Issue:** They routinely group datasets like `dc`, `moc22`, and `a549` into a single massive cluster simply because their global statistical variances are similarly large compared to the rest of the datasets. 
*   **DINOv2 Impact:** For DINOv2, MMD-RBF blurs almost all 13 primary datasets into a single cluster, making it impossible to establish a targeted fine-tuning strategy.

### The Ground Truth: Coverage Distance & FID
*   **Behavior:** FID incorporates covariance geometry strictly, while **Coverage Distance** acts as an asymmetric, non-parametric geometric check (measuring if individual data points from Dataset A actually sit next to data points from Dataset B in the manifold).
*   **The Correction:** Coverage Distance dismantles the false MMD clusters. It proves that while `dc`, `a549`, and `moc22` have similar global shapes, their local individual features **never physically intersect**. Coverage correctly identifies that `a549` actually belongs with the Epithelial cluster, while `dc` and `moc22` are isolated domains.

## 3. General Consensus on Cell-Line Clusters

By prioritizing **Coverage Distance**, we arrive at a strict consensus on the true morphological clusters. (Note: While RF-DETR enforces these boundaries strictly, DINOv2 fundamentally agrees with these core families when using geometric metrics like Coverage).

1.  **The Epithelial / Carcinoma Super-Cluster**
    *   *Datasets:* `mc38`, `hela`, `c8d1a_astrocytes`, `a549`
    *   *Consensus:* `mc38` acts as the massive central root. Its feature space broadly covers most of these cell lines.
2.  **The Fibroblast-Like Super-Cluster**
    *   *Datasets:* `preadipocytes`, `hs675tfibroblasts`, `imr90`
    *   *Consensus:* `preadipocytes` provides deep, dense coverage over this entire cluster, acting as the parent manifold.
3.  **The Dendritic Cell (DC) Cluster**
    *   *Datasets:* `dc`
    *   *Consensus:* Highly separate and distinct from all other clusters.
4.  **The U87 / MC38-Variant Cluster**
    *   *Datasets:* `u87`, `mc38_0625` batch
    *   *Consensus:* These two form a tight, isolated cluster across almost all metrics, diverging slightly from the main Epithelial trunk.
5.  **The Isolates**
    *   *Datasets:* `moc22`, `enteric-glia`
    *   *Consensus:* These datasets appear distinctly different from the major clusters. They do not share sufficient local geometric overlap with any other cell line to be safely "covered" by proxy.

## 4. Proposed Datasets for Finetuning (The 6-Centroids)

To achieve maximum zero-shot generalizability while fine-tuning on the fewest datasets possible, we must select one representative anchor from each of the distinct geometric regions identified above. 

**The Minimal Finetuning Set (N = 6):**
1.  **`mc38`** *(Represents the Epithelial/Carcinoma super-cluster)*
2.  **`preadipocytes`** *(Represents the Fibroblast super-cluster)*
3.  **`dc`** *(Represents the Dendritic Cell cluster)*
4.  **`u87`** *(Represents the U87/MC38-variant cluster)*
5.  **`moc22`** *(Isolate)*
6.  **`enteric-glia`** *(Extreme Isolate)*

Fine-tuning on these 6 centroids will inject the necessary representational variance to successfully generalize to the held-out `hela`, `astrocytes`, `a549`, `fibroblasts`, and `imr90` lineages zero-shot.

## 5. Training Subsets & Generalizability Experiments

Because **Coverage Distance** is asymmetric, we can mathematically define strict subsets where $X \in Y$ means "Training on $Y$ provides coverage over $X$". 

Based on Coverage at $K=10$, we extract the following hierarchical chains. These chains serve as the ultimate blueprint for empirical generalizability tests: training on the broader parent will easily cover the leaf, but training on the narrow leaf will fail to generalize to the parent.

*   **The Fibroblast Chain:**
    `Hs675Tfibroblasts` $\in$ `preadipocytes_caged` $\in$ `preadipocytes_uncaged`
*   **The Epithelial / U87 Chain:**
    `mc38_caged_0624` $\in$ `mc38_caged_0625` $\in$ `u87_caged` $\in$ `mc38_uncaged_0624`
*   **The Isolate Transition Chain:**
    `mc38_caged_0624` $\in$ `a549` $\in$ `moc22`
*   **The Dendritic Cell Chain:**
    `mc38_caged_0624` $\in$ `DC_caged_0516` $\in$ `DC_caged_0515`

*(Note: `mc38_caged_0624` acts as the ultimate narrow leaf/subset in multiple chains, proving its distribution is highly specific and central, whereas `mc38_uncaged` acts as a massive global root covering huge swaths of the feature space.)*
